## Three nested geometry families

For a finite sphere-refinement floor L, a full local address is:

~~~text
(omega_L, sphere_cell, sector_12, apex_6, vertex_8,
 lane, epoch, glyph_level, language, question_index)
~~~

The three geometry families remain typed:

1. **Six-apex cubic body.** Six square pyramids meet at a center; its
   representation orbit has eight C2^3 vertices, twelve undirected cube edges,
   and six square face cycles.
2. **Twelve-directed tetrahedral body.** A tetrahedron has six undirected edges
   and twelve directed transitions. This is not automatically the same object
   as the cube's twelve face-triangulation sectors.
3. **Pi-limit spherical family.** At level L the chosen icosphere refinement has
   F_L = 20 times 4^L triangular faces, E_L = 30 times 4^L edges, and
   V_L = 10 times 4^L + 2 vertices. Pi governs continuum angle and measure; it
   is not a literal finite side count.

The finite geometry-only address capacity at floor L is:

~~~text
F_L times 12 times 6 times 8
~~~

At L=4 this is 5,120 times 12 times 6 times 8 = 2,949,120 local geometry
addresses before lanes, epochs, glyph levels, languages, questions, agents, and
floors are included. Increasing L provides arbitrarily fine finite floors; it
does not claim infinitely many simultaneously materialized processes.

The genesis model is finite at every receipt: an explicit empty-state
commitment parents the first namespace seed; finite collectors, catalogs, and
slice Omegas may then be added. An OmniGNN is the designed graph between those
finite slice Omegas. Counts such as 50 or 1,500 resident slices remain capacity
choices until a measured runtime receipt materializes them. “Infinity” names
the continuation rule for computing another address or refinement, not an
artifact containing infinity itself.

## The omnicentric law

For cube index vectors in (Z/2Z)^3, recentering at vertex v is:

~~~text
T_v(x) = x XOR v
~~~

It maps v to zero and preserves Hamming distance and adjacency. This is why
every node can see itself as a local center without erasing the parent
hierarchy. Payload bytes are transformed only by their separately proved
representation action; the coordinate law is not permission to blindly XOR
data.

An improvement claim requires the strict lineage comparison:

~~~text
delta_n = Eval_heldout(F_(n+1)) - Eval_heldout(F_n) > 0
~~~

The parent and child PIDs, unchanged held-out set, leakage audit, evaluator

The held-out-set SHA-256 and evaluator SHA-256 are committed before child
generation. Stochastic evaluations additionally record seeds, repeated trials,
and confidence bounds; the measured delta must exceed evaluation noise.

Glyph-language improvement has five independent gates: it must exceed symbol
substitution, compose novel glyph messages, reverse exactly between native and

language after alignment.
