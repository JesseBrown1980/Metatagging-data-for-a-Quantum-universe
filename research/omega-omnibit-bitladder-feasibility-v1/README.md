# Omega-omnibit / bit-ladder feasibility survey (v1)

This is a **cold, additive research index** — a feasibility survey, not an
implementation. It evaluates whether the operator's new design idea (a
native 2/4/8/16/32/64-bit floor ladder continuing into the existing
256/1024/4096 glyph floors, bridged by the Q-Prism 3D-slice mechanism, fused
via a "quanted omega omnibit" transition into one shared root, with a
quantum-encrypted-cloning-inspired single-use-key analogue for family
access) is architecturally sound, before anything is built.

## Method

40 parallel agents, each briefed on (a) the new arXiv:2602.10695v1 encrypted
single-use quantum cloning paper — measured on real IBM Heron R2 hardware,
newer than any training-data cutoff, with its exact hard boundary stated so
no agent extends it past what the paper actually shows — and (b) this
system's own two prior MEASURED NEGATIVE results (unified-omega-v1 core:
bpc 4.14 vs 2.65; band-ladder omnibit: 5.7x worse than flat), scouted real,
checkable GitHub/arXiv prior art across five angles (bit-depth ladders,
Q-Prism/shadow-reconstruction analogues, omega-family/GNN unification,
the quantum-cloning-paper-grounded angle, and general content-addressed
multiresolution systems). A synthesis pass merged all 40 reports; three
independent adversarial skeptics then tried to refute every claim in the
synthesis; a final pass corrected every issue the skeptics found (full
changelog in `FEASIBILITY-REPORT.md` Section 9) rather than just noting it.

Independently, the LIRIS seat reviewed the same proposal from first
principles the same day and converged on the same discipline from a
different angle (typed-lattice framing, proven commutative diagrams
required before any "unified omnibook" claim) — folded into
`FEASIBILITY-REPORT.md` Section 10, with the convergence made explicit.

## Verdict

**POSSIBLE_WITH_CAVEATS.** Full detail, all citations, and the complete
claims-gate tag ledger (MEASURED / CANON / UNVERIFIED, kept strictly
separate) are in `FEASIBILITY-REPORT.md`. Short version:

- The native bit-depth rungs are sound **only** as native-integer-width
  block packing (Parquet/ORC/PFOR-style) — **not** as a continuation of the
  glyph-codebook (bijective relabeling) mechanism, which would very likely
  repeat the already-measured failure direction.
- Q-Prism bridging and omega-root fusion are well-grounded as
  addressing/commitment mechanisms — never as compression.
- A literal static, non-interactive, no-trust-anchor single-use gate
  between families is **proven impossible** (Broadbent-Gutoski-Stebila,
  CRYPTO 2013) — the quantum-cloning analogy holds only as metaphor for
  graded/threshold access, for which real classical primitives already
  exist (WOTS+, compartmented secret sharing).
- The R/N/Q colored-pole axis and the mirror/nullspace circular-lensing
  idea got no scout coverage — genuinely `UNVERIFIED`, not evaluated either
  way.
