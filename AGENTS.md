# Repository evidence discipline

- Keep conceptual metadata, deterministic simulation, external physical experiments, and Asolaria hardware deployment as separate evidence layers.
- Never make the blanket claim that physical quantum cloning is impossible. arXiv:2602.10695v1 reports a measured physical IBM Heron-R2 demonstration of encrypted cloning with a single-use quantum decryption key.
- Preserve the protocol boundary: one selected encrypted clone can be decrypted; key use leaves the other encrypted clones indecipherable. This is not unrestricted simultaneously readable plaintext cloning.
- Do not present classical branch copies, Q-Prism simulation, prismed-laser optics, arbitrary-particle replication, UAP identity, or cosmological interpretation as measured by that paper.
- Receipt files must be LF-pinned and independently checked by HBP/HBI/SHA verification before publication.
