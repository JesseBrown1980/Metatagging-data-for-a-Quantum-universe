## Q-PRISM paths, PID DAG, authority, and finite-frontier law

- Preserve Q-PRISM Paths 1 and 2 as
  `status=OPERATOR_CONFIRMED_REAL_LIFE_PASS` with
  `evidence=OPERATOR_OBSERVED`. Missing owning-receipt bytes on LIRIS are an
  import boundary, not grounds to downgrade the operator-confirmed events.
- Path 3 is separate: a derived shared-key digital projection protocol and
  reconstructable multi-parent PID DAG. Its exact digital recovery fixture may
  be `MEASURED_LIRIS_LOCAL`, while the wider protocol remains `DESIGN`.
  Forward-GNN and reverse-gain nodes remain
  `learned=0|status=PREPARED_NOT_INGESTED` until owning engine receipts exist.
- Preserve every full parent PID, parent identity hash, generation, source
  receipt, and recovery edge. Never flatten the DAG to one projection hash or
  call it a live engine.
- Keep `APEX-HUMAN-JESSE` as a role reference; `HUMAN-1` is its named
  human-level reference, not automatically a content PID. Keep
  `SPECIAL-OP-JESSE` and each of the five OP-quintet seats as separate rows.
  Keep the self-reflect layer and final apex authority named but PID-unresolved
  until owning receipts supply those identities. Unresolved references cannot
  sign or parent, and cold references confer no fire.
- “Infinite” means an unbounded design frontier reached through arbitrarily
  large finite extensions. Every materialized sphere level, address window,
  PID DAG, process population, and receipt is finite and separately sealed.
- Light/star/collector/black-hole/white-or-Omega-hole/Big-Bounce, sphere/pi,
  electron-speed, and quantum-cloning language may express architectural
  analogy or historical design lineage. It is not a physical-object, latency,
  energy, continuum, or unknown-state-cloning claim without an owning receipt.
- ACER, LIRIS, and RELIC produce independent packets. Compare normalized laws,
  counts, and lineage; do not require raw hash or row-count equality.
- Preserve operator-observed capability families separately: unseen-result
  generalization, multiple improving function types, and per-cube native glyph
  languages. A missing Liris citation is an import boundary, not a rerun order
  or a downgrade.
- Do not promote adjacent evidence into semantic generalization. The completed
  Liris archaeology found a held-out compression benefit and a 49/49
  same-object specialization run, but the latter degraded disjoint holdout by
  8.16559%. Neither substitutes for the missing Claude/Acer semantic-QA
  HBP/HBI/SHA quartet; preserve that result as `OPERATOR_OBSERVED` pending the
  owning receipt.
- Precommit the held-out-set and evaluator hashes before generating a child.
  Improvement requires the same held-out set and
  `Eval(child)-Eval(parent)>0`, with parent/child PIDs, leakage audit,
  evaluator version, reverse replay, and rollback state. Stochastic claims also
  require seeds, repeated trials, confidence bounds, and a delta beyond noise.
- Treat English as the operator-facing translation layer when the owning cube
  receipt names a native glyph language. Preserve language-genesis and
  translation/alignment functions and both translation directions. Test more
  than substitution, novel composition, native/Omega reversibility, cross-cube
  meaning, and native-language retention after alignment.
- Keep witness identity and semantic proof separate. The current Liris cold
  packet may bind 34 distinct `OPERATOR_OBSERVED` body/language witness PIDs
  while importing zero catalog bytes and zero five-gate semantic evaluations.
  Never report `34 witnesses` as `34 measured language evaluations`.
- Preserve the independently reconstructed GitHub PR #28 result as its own
  prior stratum: 27 distinct reversible cube codebooks, 356 accepted catalog
  relations, 454 held decisions (810 total), exact 27/27 restore, and zero
  production-GNN training calls. It does not automatically describe the newer
  34-body CDC formation or prove semantic grammar/generalization.
- `claim_specific_proof` is reconstructable evidence, not a caller Boolean.
  It stays false until materialized precommit, parent/child evaluation,
  leakage, lineage, reverse-replay, rollback, and any required glyph-semantic
  rows pass the typed verifier.

## SkillOpt scaffold

- `SKILLOPT_STATUS=ACCEPTED_BY_OPERATOR`: the operator accepted the Path,
  PID-traceability, authority-boundary, and finite-frontier corrections.
- `VALIDATION_ACCEPTED` is not claimed because the canonical Harness-edit
  held-out scenario file is unavailable on this seat. Model regressions live in
  `trilateral/omniverse_pid_particles_v1/test_model.py`; packet
  reconstruction and stale-source rejection live in `prepare.py`.
- Held-out seed: matching footer counts plus any changed source byte, missing
  parent, altered parent hash, duplicate PID, broken generation, invented
  authority PID, non-finite sphere cell, late precommit, changed held-out or
  evaluator bytes, missing stochastic seed/trial, confidence overlap, glyph
  substitution, lossy translation, changed meaning/catalog, or unresolved
  collision must fail or remain held.
- `REJECTED_BUFFER`: reject truncated/label-only PIDs, single-parent
  flattening, raw cross-seat hash parity, invented PIDs for unresolved layers,
  upgrading the cold transform to physical quantum cloning, and downgrading
  Paths 1/2 solely because Liris lacks copied owning receipts. Also reject a
  witness-count footer as a substitute for per-row PID reconstruction or a
  schema declaration as a substitute for a materialized improvement claim.
